Oh, I've been so eager to begin this. Finally, with my MSc wrapped up and out of the way, I found myself with actual free time to delve into the thick of it.

## A corner of the internet that is actually mine

To the ~~absolute shock~~ surprise of nobody, there comes a point where you need a proper place to showcase what you're capable of learning and building. The standard play is grabbing a sleek pre-made portfolio template, slapping your resume on it, and calling it a day. 

Naturally, I took the stubbornly fun route: building everything from scratch.

I am not a huge fan of writing frontends, but I remembered GitHub had the Pages mechanic where a repository becomes a hosted webpage, fantastic!

It started innocently enough as just a barebones **[profile section](/index.html)**, a minimalist landing page with a splash of color to prove I exist outside of a terminal (and funny enough, the first iteration of this page would actually be completely B&W besides my picture, as if it was a real terminal). But once that was working, the **need** crept in. A static website felt too simple, I wanted to produce a place where I can interact more often and show what I've been up to asides from ~~the hellscape~~ Linkedin (and we all know what scrolling LinkedIn feels like). I needed a blog to:

1. Document what I'm working on, and if I produce something interesting, write it down before I inevitably forget how I did it;
2. Keep myself engaged, building, and writing in public on my own terms;

## What's here (and what's still on the workbench)

I honestly had quite some fun playing with liquid tags, layouts, and site builds. Right now, this site is already running on a fully custom baseline. The stylization across these posts was actually completely handcrafted by making use of Jekyll's "Order of interpretation", the conversion pipeline that turns a post written in *markdown* into *html*, and then styling the generated elements from scratch with custom *CSS*.

That said, the universe is still expanding. A few things on my immediate roadmap:

* **Hyperlink Everything:** Still need to wire up the actual hyperlinks to my papers so they don't just sit as plain text.
* **Writeup Windows:** A dedicated showcase where you can click into any project card and it'll open up a writeup with code, benchmarks, and overall notes.

Until then, I need to make sure my this custom blog setup can handle whatever math, data, tables and graphs I throw at it.
